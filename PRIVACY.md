
Privacy Policy of SuitIAPro

Thank you for using SuitIAPro. This policy explains how we handle your device permissions and data. Our core principle is privacy: all artificial intelligence processing takes place directly on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your photos, audio, or documents on any external server.
1. Information We Process and Device Permissions
SuitIAPro requests access to certain features of your device solely to provide you with its local AI functionalities. Below, we detail the specific use of each permission:
Camera
Purpose: The camera is used only for two purposes:
Image analysis: Take a real-time photo so the AI can process it and provide you with an analysis or answer about its content.
Document scanning: Capture documents to convert them into editable text or analyze their information.
Processing: The captured image is sent directly to the local AI engine on your device. It is not saved to your photo gallery unless you choose to do so, and it is not uploaded to the cloud or shared with third parties.
Microphone
Purpose: The microphone is used exclusively for the speech-to-text feature, which allows you to dictate your queries to the AI instead of typing them.
Processing: The captured audio is converted to text on your device, and that text is processed locally. No voice recording is stored. The audio never leaves your device.
Storage / Photos / Files
Purpose: We request storage access so you can select existing content and use it with the AI:
Pick a photo from your gallery: If you prefer not to use the camera, you can select a saved image for the AI to analyze.
Load a local AI model: If you already have an AI model saved on your device, you can tell the app to use it directly without needing a new download.
Browse documents for analysis: Allows you to select files (PDF, text, etc.) from your device so the AI can process them and provide you with information or summaries.
Processing: The files you select are processed entirely on your device. The app does not copy, move, or transmit your files to any external location.
2. Personal Data and Storage
No profiles created: SuitIAPro does not require you to create an account, nor does it collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information for its basic functionality.
Local history: Your conversation history with the AI and the app settings are stored exclusively in your device's local memory. You can delete this history at any time from the app settings.
No invasive analytics: We do not use third-party SDKs that track your activity or collect device usage data.
3. Data Sharing
We do not share or sell any personal data. Because all processing is entirely on-device, we have no access to your photos, audio, documents, or the content of your queries. No information leaves your device to servers operated by us or by third parties.
4. Security
Since all your data resides on your device, security largely depends on the protection measures you have configured (screen lock, biometrics, system encryption). We are committed to designing the app following best practices to minimize resource access and ensure secure, isolated processing.
5. Changes to This Policy
Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
